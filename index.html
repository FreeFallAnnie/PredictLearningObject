WonderSpark • Learning object

Accept a small quest: a physical cue supports the transition from intention to task initiation. Explore three ways to organise what to do next, then practise beginning.

Prototype boundaries: simulated wizard-based AR cue; rule-based task suggestions and estimates (not live AI); session-only demonstration counters (not consent-based or persistent analytics).

Learning design

Mindful agency and self-regulation: the learner selects a pathway, chooses and adapts a first action, and reflects on their experience. Gradual release aims toward independent initiation. The guiding wizard is a personally meaningful mentor cue. Quests encourage participation without streaks or penalties; abandoning a quest is permitted. Reflection and optional check-ins support evaluation without penalising incomplete work.
